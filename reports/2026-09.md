### 1. Atlas Copco Varisco deploys Physics AI for industrial pump impeller design

- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Entity:** Atlas Copco Varisco; SimScale

Data-driven CFD surrogate trained on about 600 simulations with coverage check and full-solver fallback It integrates with CFTurbo; Onshape; SimScale Physics AI; full CFD fallback. A production-oriented surrogate workflow replaced a serial CAD-to-CFD loop and exposed an explicit fallback path when the AI model lacks coverage. The source reports Design cycle from 3-5 weeks to days; about 5 seconds per design point; 75-iteration optimization under 3 hours. All quantified results come from the supplier case page; benchmark definitions, error distribution, design-space boundaries and independent replication are not disclosed.

**Sources:** [Primary source](https://www.simscale.com/press/atlas-copco-cuts-industrial-pump-design-cycles/)

### 2. Ford study uses symbolic regression as a real-time brake-temperature virtual sensor

- **Date:** 2026-09-14
- **Entity:** Ford Motor Company; SAE paper authors

The authors use physics-informed symbolic regression to derive a real-time virtual-sensor equation and test it on vehicles. Error rises with more demanding duty cycles, providing a useful condition-dependent view rather than a single headline metric. Public abstract does not disclose fleet size, vehicle transfer, sensor uncertainty, extreme-temperature behavior or production readiness.

**Sources:** [Primary source](https://saemobilus.sae.org/papers/real-time-estimation-brake-temperature-using-physics-informed-machine-learning-model-2026-01-0810)

### 3. Physics-informed ML Digital Twin estimates clamp force and friction in electro-mechanical brakes

- **Date:** 2026-09-14
- **Entity:** SAE technical paper authors

The method preserves governing actuator/caliper dynamics and learns residual effects such as damping, friction, hysteresis and backlash from test-stand signals. The work is a concrete hybrid-model example tied to a physical brake rig rather than a generic Digital Twin claim. No numeric accuracy, robustness range, cross-unit transfer or real-time compute information is visible in the public abstract.

**Sources:** [Primary source](https://saemobilus.sae.org/papers/physics-informed-machine-learning-digital-twin-electro-mechanical-brakes-clamp-force-friction-estimation-2026-01-0806)

### 5. Brake-pad wear model reports high global accuracy but weak within-regime forecasting

- **Date:** 2026-09-14
- **Entity:** Southern Illinois University; SAE paper authors

The study combines boosted trees and residual neural correction, with stratification, sample reweighting and hyperparameter optimization. The sharp disagreement between global R2 and regime/persistence tests is more informative than the headline score and exposes data scarcity in severe regimes. Only 280 cycles, simplified protocol and one experimental setup; formulation transfer and full-duty-cycle generalization are unproven.

**Sources:** [Primary source](https://saemobilus.sae.org/papers/predicting-wear-nao-brake-pad-using-scaled-fmvss-135-testing-machine-learning-2026-01-0808)

- **Entity:** SAE technical paper authors

### 9. ChronosGD uses frozen time-series embeddings for traceable geometric-deviation estimation

- **Date:** 2026-09-22
- **Entity:** SAE technical paper authors

The method embeds current and historical process windows with a frozen foundation model, retrieves similar cases and weights their measured deviations. Retrieval supplies explicit neighbor provenance and avoids gradient retraining, shifting the design toward decision traceability. Public abstract does not expose dataset size, numeric accuracy, drift behavior or comparison against tuned supervised baselines.

**Sources:** [Primary source](https://saemobilus.sae.org/papers/decision-centric-virtual-metrology-geometric-deviation-via-foundation-embedding-retrieval-2026-01-7554)

### 10. Surrogate comparison exposes accuracy and physical-plausibility tradeoffs in thermoelectric multiphysics modeling

- **Date:** 2026-09
- **Entity:** Energy and AI journal authors

The study trains three surrogate families on FEM-generated data at increasing sample sizes and checks held-out and experimental behavior. Large speedups coexist with meaningful error and different failure modes; the explicit negative-output example shows why average accuracy alone is insufficient. Narrow device and parameter domain, small held-out set and no production decision study; issue-month inclusion may follow an earlier online-first date.

**Sources:** [Primary source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666546826001382)
